Countable vs. Uncountable Nouns

Countable Nouns

You can count them with numbers. They have singular and plural forms.

🍎 1 apple → 3 apples
🚗 1 car → 2 cars
🐕 1 dog → 4 dogs
Uncountable Nouns

You cannot count them one by one (liquids, food mass, concepts). No plural "-s"!

💧 Water, Milk, Coffee (liquids)
🍚 Rice, Bread, Cheese (food mass)
💰 Money, Time, Music (ideas)
2

Plural Noun Spelling Rules

Most Nouns (+S)

book → books
cat → cats
pen → pens

Ends -ch, -sh, -x, -s (+ES)

bus → buses
box → boxes
watch → watches

Consonant + Y (drop Y → -IES)

baby → babies
city → cities
story → stories

⚠️ Irregular Plurals

child → children
person → people
tooth → teeth

3

Quantifiers Guide

Quantifier Use With Example
Some / Any Both Countable & Uncountable "I have some apples." / "Is there any juice?"
Many Countable Plural Nouns "How many books do you have?"
Much Uncountable Nouns "We don't have much water."
A few / A little Countable (few) / Uncountable (little) "A few cookies" / "A little sugar"
A lot of Both (large quantity) "She has a lot of friends and money!"
